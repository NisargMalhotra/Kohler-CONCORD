# Kohler Products Catalog

## CS-PUB-001: Faucets Catalog
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler offers a wide range of premium faucets for kitchens and bathrooms, combining innovative technology with timeless design. The Sensate Touchless kitchen faucet features state-of-the-art response technology that responds to your every move, making kitchen tasks more efficient and hygienic. Our Barossa line offers sleek, contemporary styling with easy-to-use pull-down spray heads. For commercial and heavy-use environments, the Triton Bowe series provides unmatched durability and reliability. Standard models come with detailed specifications including spout reach, height, and mounting requirements. Many of our faucets are WaterSense certified, ensuring maximum water savings without compromising performance. Flow rates typically range from 1.2 to 1.5 gallons per minute (gpm) for bathroom sink faucets and 1.5 to 1.8 gpm for kitchen faucets, meeting stringent conservation standards.

## CS-PUB-002: Toilets Catalog
<!-- access_level: public -->
Experience the pinnacle of bathroom comfort and hygiene with Kohler's diverse range of toilets. The Numi smart toilet is our flagship model, offering personalized settings, heated seating, advanced bidet functionality, and ambient lighting, all controlled via remote or the KOHLER Konnect app. Our standard High-Efficiency Toilets (HET) provide exceptional performance while conserving water. Kohler's proprietary AquaPiston technology delivers a fast, powerful, and plug-free flush by allowing water to flow into the bowl from all sides. The Class Five flushing system uses the force of gravity and a precision-engineered tank, bowl, and trapway to create a strong siphon during flushing. Gallons per flush (gpf) ratings vary across models, with many options available at 1.28 gpf for optimal water conservation and WaterSense certification.

## CS-PUB-003: Showers & Bath
<!-- access_level: public -->
Transform your daily routine into a spa-like experience with Kohler's showering and bathing solutions. The DTV+ digital showering system allows you to create customized water, light, sound, and steam experiences with an intuitive touchscreen interface. Our extensive range of showerheads includes single-function, multi-function, rainheads, and handshowers to suit any preference. Kohler is committed to sustainability; a significant portion of our showerheads carry the EPA's WaterSense label. These eco-friendly options typically feature flow rates of 1.75 to 2.0 gallons per minute (gpm), providing a satisfying spray while using significantly less water than standard 2.5 gpm models. Our bathing selection ranges from classic enameled cast iron tubs to modern acrylic freestanding baths, offering relaxation and elegance for any bathroom design.

## CS-PUB-004: Generators
<!-- access_level: public -->
Ensure your home remains powered during unexpected outages with Kohler home standby generators. Designed for reliability and performance, our generators automatically restore power to your home within seconds of an outage. We offer a variety of sizes and power capacities, from 10kW to 20kW models suitable for standard residential needs, up to larger industrial-grade units for complete home and business protection. Specifications include detailed footprints, sound levels, and power output ratings. Kohler generators are versatile and can run on different fuel types, including natural gas and liquid propane, depending on your home's setup and local availability. All models are enclosed in weather-resistant, corrosion-proof housing to withstand harsh environmental conditions and ensure long-lasting durability.

## CS-PUB-005: Smart Home & KOHLER Konnect
<!-- access_level: public -->
The KOHLER Konnect ecosystem brings intelligent control and personalization to your bathroom and kitchen fixtures. Using the KOHLER Konnect app, you can easily set preferences, monitor water usage, and control compatible products right from your smartphone. The ecosystem is designed to seamlessly integrate into your existing smart home setup. Compatible products include the Sensate touchless faucet, Numi smart toilet, DTV+ digital showering system, and PerfectFill smart bathing technology. For hands-free convenience, KOHLER Konnect features voice control compatibility with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. This allows you to start your shower, flush the toilet, or dispense specific amounts of water simply by using voice commands, enhancing the overall functionality and luxury of your home.

## CS-PUB-006: Water Conservation Features
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler is dedicated to promoting sustainability through advanced water conservation features across our product lines. A large number of our toilets, faucets, and showerheads have earned the EPA's WaterSense certification, meaning they use at least 20% less water than standard models while maintaining superior performance. We utilize low-flow aerators in our faucets to reduce water consumption without sacrificing water pressure. Our touchless technology, available in faucets and toilets, minimizes water waste by ensuring the fixture only runs when in use. Additionally, the H2Wise smart home water monitor, powered by Phyn, attaches to your main water line to track household water usage, identify potential leaks, and provide insights to help you save water and protect your home from water damage.
