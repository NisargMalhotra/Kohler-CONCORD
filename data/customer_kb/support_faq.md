# Support and FAQ

## CS-FAQ-001: How to Contact Kohler Support
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler offers multiple ways for customers to reach our dedicated Customer Care team. You can contact us by phone toll-free at 1-800-4KOHLER (1-800-456-4537) for assistance with product selection, installation, and warranty claims. Our phone support hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time. For non-urgent inquiries, you can email us through the contact form available on the Kohler website, and a representative will respond within 24-48 business hours. We also offer a Live Chat feature on KOHLER.com during regular business hours for quick questions and immediate assistance. For support related to KOHLER Konnect smart products, specialized technical assistance is available through the app's help section.

## CS-FAQ-002: Shipping & Delivery
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler provides reliable shipping options to ensure your products arrive safely and on time. Standard parcel shipments for smaller items typically arrive within 3-5 business days after processing. Larger items, such as bathtubs, vanities, and generators, are shipped via LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) freight and usually require a scheduled delivery appointment. Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number so you can monitor its progress online. If your shipment arrives damaged, please refuse the delivery if possible, or note the damage on the delivery receipt and contact Kohler Customer Care immediately. We require all damage claims to be reported within 48 hours of receipt to expedite the replacement process.

## CS-FAQ-003: Installation Guidelines
<!-- access_level: public -->
Proper installation is crucial for the optimal performance and longevity of your Kohler products. While many of our simpler faucets and accessories can be successfully installed by a confident DIYer following the provided instructions, we highly recommend professional installation for complex items like smart toilets, DTV+ showering systems, and generators. Detailed installation guides and rough-in requirement sheets are available on the product pages at KOHLER.com to help you prepare your space. For professionals and contractors, we offer a dedicated Pro Support line for technical assistance and troubleshooting during installation. Always consult local plumbing and electrical codes to ensure your installation meets all regulatory requirements.

## CS-FAQ-004: Parts & Replacement
<!-- access_level: public -->
Finding the right replacement parts for your Kohler product begins with identifying the correct model number and, in some cases, the serial number. This information is typically located on a sticker inside the toilet tank, on the cold water supply line of a faucet, or on the back of the product. Once you have the model number, you can use the interactive parts catalogs on the Kohler website to find the specific components you need. Genuine Kohler replacement parts can be ordered directly through KOHLER.com or purchased at authorized Kohler dealers and distributors. Using non-OEM parts can affect product performance and may void your warranty, so we always recommend using genuine Kohler components.

## CS-FAQ-005: Privacy & Data
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data, especially concerning our smart home products. The KOHLER Konnect app collects usage data necessary for product functionality, such as water consumption metrics and personalized presets, which are securely stored on cloud servers. Users have the option to opt-out of certain data collection for marketing purposes within the app's privacy settings. If you wish to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data, you can submit a formal request through the privacy portal on KOHLER.com. We fully comply with data protection regulations, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), providing you with rights to access, correct, or delete your information.

## CS-FAQ-006: Financing & Payment
<!-- access_level: public -->
To make your dream bathroom or kitchen a reality, Kohler offers flexible financing and payment options. Purchases made directly on KOHLER.com can be paid for using major credit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover. We also partner with third-party financing providers to offer installment plans for qualifying customers, allowing you to spread the cost of your purchase over time with competitive interest rates. Information about financing options and eligibility requirements can be found during the checkout process online. Additionally, keep an eye out for seasonal promotional offers and special financing deals that may be available for a limited time on select products or services.
