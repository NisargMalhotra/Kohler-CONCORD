# Warranty and Returns

## CS-WRT-001: Warranty Policy
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler Co. stands behind the quality of its products with comprehensive warranty policies designed to give you peace of mind. Our residential cast iron and vitreous china fixtures, as well as many of our faucets, are covered by a Lifetime Limited Warranty, ensuring they are free of defects in material and workmanship during normal residential use. Electronic and smart home products, including those in the KOHLER Konnect ecosystem, typically come with a 3-Year Limited Warranty. Kohler home standby generators are backed by a robust 5-Year or 2000-Hour Limited Warranty, whichever comes first, covering parts, labor, and travel for the initial years. To file a warranty claim, customers should contact the Kohler Customer Care Center with proof of purchase and a description of the issue. Claims can be submitted via phone, email, or through our online support portal.

## CS-WRT-002: Returns & Exchanges
<!-- access_level: public -->
We want you to be completely satisfied with your Kohler purchase. If you need to return or exchange an item, you have a 45-day window from the date of delivery to do so for a refund or replacement. Please note that a 15% restocking fee may apply to certain returns, and original shipping charges are generally non-refundable. Custom orders, personalized items, and products that have been installed or altered are non-returnable. To initiate a return or exchange, you must first obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number from our Customer Care team. Items must be returned in their original, uninstalled condition with all original packaging and accessories. Once the returned item is received and inspected at our facility, refunds are typically processed within 7-10 business days to the original method of payment.

## CS-WRT-003: Product Registration
<!-- access_level: public -->
Registering your Kohler product is a quick and easy process that provides numerous benefits. You can complete your product registration online at KOHLER.com/register or through the KOHLER Konnect app for smart products. Registering your product helps us provide you with faster and more efficient customer support if you ever need to file a warranty claim. It also allows us to send you important product updates, maintenance tips, and safety notices related to your specific model. Additionally, registered users gain exclusive access to special promotions, new product announcements, and helpful resources tailored to their purchases. We highly recommend registering your product within 30 days of purchase or installation to maximize these benefits and ensure your warranty information is accurately recorded in our system.

## CS-WRT-004: What Warranty Does NOT Cover
<!-- access_level: public -->
While Kohler warranties are comprehensive, there are certain situations and types of damage that are not covered. The warranty does not cover damage resulting from improper installation, failure to follow installation instructions, or modifications made to the product. Damage caused by aggressive water conditions, harsh cleaning chemicals, or abrasive materials is also excluded. The residential warranty does not apply to products used in commercial settings; a separate commercial warranty applies in those cases. Cosmetic damage resulting from normal wear and tear, such as scratching, staining, or fading over time, is not covered. Finally, the warranty does not cover labor charges, installation, or other incidental or consequential costs unless explicitly stated in the specific product's warranty document.
