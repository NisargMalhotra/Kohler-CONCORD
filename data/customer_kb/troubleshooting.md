# Troubleshooting Guide

## CS-TSH-001: Faucet Troubleshooting
<!-- access_level: public -->
If your Kohler faucet is leaking from the spout or handle, the most common culprit is a worn-out cartridge or O-ring, which can usually be easily replaced with standard tools. Low water pressure from the faucet often indicates a clogged aerator; try unscrewing the aerator and soaking it in a vinegar solution to dissolve mineral deposits. If your pull-down or pull-out sprayer is not retracting properly, ensure the hose weight is correctly positioned and that there are no obstructions under the sink. For touchless faucets that are not responding, first check the batteries or power supply, and ensure the sensor lens is clean and free of water spots. Detailed cartridge replacement instructions and part numbers can be found in your specific model's installation guide or on the Kohler parts website.

## CS-TSH-002: Smart Fixture Troubleshooting
<!-- access_level: public -->
When experiencing connectivity drops with your KOHLER Konnect smart fixtures, start by restarting your home Wi-Fi router and ensuring the fixture is within range of a strong signal. If voice control via Alexa or Google Assistant fails, verify that the KOHLER Konnect skill is properly linked in the respective smart home app and that your voice assistant device is online. For app pairing issues during initial setup, ensure your smartphone's Bluetooth is enabled and that you are connected to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network, as 5GHz networks are often not supported. If a touchless sensor is not responding, try cleaning the sensor area with a soft, damp cloth and checking for any software updates available through the KOHLER Konnect app. If problems persist, a factory reset of the fixture may be necessary.

## CS-TSH-003: Toilet Troubleshooting
<!-- access_level: public -->
A running toilet is typically caused by a faulty flapper or a misadjusted fill valve, both of which are inexpensive and straightforward to replace. If you are experiencing a weak or sluggish flush, check that the water level in the tank is set to the correct waterline mark and ensure the rim jets inside the bowl are not clogged with mineral buildup. "Phantom flushing," where the toilet seems to flush itself periodically, is usually a sign of a slow leak from the tank into the bowl, often due to a deteriorated flapper or seal. If the fill valve fails to shut off or makes excessive noise, it may be clogged with debris from the water line and should be flushed out or replaced entirely. Always shut off the water supply valve behind the toilet before attempting any internal tank repairs.

## CS-TSH-004: Generator Troubleshooting
<!-- access_level: public -->
If your Kohler home standby generator fails to start during a power outage, first check the main line circuit breaker and ensure the controller is set to "AUTO" mode. Review the digital controller display for any specific error codes, which can help diagnose issues ranging from low oil pressure to battery failure. Regular maintenance is crucial; pay attention to maintenance reminders on the controller for tasks like oil changes, spark plug replacements, and air filter checks. Fuel supply issues, such as low gas pressure or a closed shut-off valve, can also prevent the generator from running and should be verified. For complex electrical or engine issues indicated by persistent fault codes, it is highly recommended to contact an authorized Kohler generator dealer for professional service.

## CS-TSH-005: When to Call a Professional
<!-- access_level: public -->
While many minor troubleshooting and maintenance tasks can be handled as DIY projects, certain situations require the expertise of a licensed professional. Any issues involving your home's main electrical system, such as wiring a new smart fixture or servicing a generator's transfer switch, should be handled by a certified electrician. Major plumbing leaks, especially those behind walls or under floors, require immediate attention from a licensed plumber to prevent extensive water damage. Furthermore, any concerns related to natural gas or liquid propane lines for your generator must be addressed by a qualified gas professional for safety and compliance with local codes. Attempting to repair these critical systems without the proper training and licenses can result in severe injury, property damage, and may void your product warranties.
