# Kohler Co. Human Resources Policy Manual

*Disclaimer: This document is synthetic and generated for Kohler Concord Enterprise AI demonstration purposes.*

## Introduction
Kohler Co. is dedicated to maintaining a positive, supportive, and dynamic workplace for all associates globally. This Human Resources Policy Manual provides comprehensive guidelines regarding employment conditions, benefits, expectations, and the rights of our associates. As a global leader in kitchen and bath products, engines, power systems, and hospitality, our workforce is our most valuable asset.

### Annual Leave (HR-POL-001)
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler Co. believes in the importance of work-life balance and encourages all associates to take regular time off to rest and recharge. 
1. Accrual Rates:
   - Full-time associates accrue 15 days of annual leave in their first year of service.
   - Upon completion of 3 years of service, the accrual rate increases to 20 days per year.
   - Upon completion of 10 years of service, the accrual rate increases to 25 days per year.
2. Carryover Policy:
   - Associates may carry over a maximum of 5 days of unused annual leave into the following calendar year.
   - Any unused leave above the 5-day limit will be forfeited on December 31st, unless prohibited by local jurisdiction laws.
3. Requesting Leave:
   - All annual leave requests must be submitted through the global HR portal (Workday) at least two weeks in advance.
   - Approval is subject to business needs and manager discretion.

### Sick Leave (HR-POL-002)
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler Co. provides paid sick leave to support associates when they are ill or need to care for a sick family member.
1. Allowance:
   - Associates are provided with 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, available on January 1st.
   - New hires receive a prorated amount based on their start date.
2. Documentation:
   - For absences of three or more consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required upon return to work.
   - Managers may request medical clearance if the illness impacts the associate's ability to perform their core duties safely.
3. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year and is not paid out upon termination of employment.

### Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-003)
<!-- access_level: public -->
To support a flexible work environment, Kohler Co. offers hybrid and remote work options for eligible roles.
1. Eligibility: Roles that do not require physical presence at manufacturing plants, design studios, or showrooms may be eligible.
2. Hybrid Schedule: The standard hybrid schedule is 3 days in the office and 2 days remote per week.
3. Equipment: Kohler Co. provides a standard IT package (laptop, monitor, headset) for remote work. Ergonomic furniture is the responsibility of the associate.

### Code of Conduct (HR-POL-004)
<!-- access_level: public -->
The Kohler Co. Code of Conduct outlines the ethical standards and behavioral expectations for all associates.
1. Integrity: We operate with absolute integrity in all our dealings, whether with customers, suppliers, or colleagues.
2. Respect: We foster an inclusive environment where everyone is treated with dignity and respect. Discrimination and harassment are strictly prohibited.
3. Sustainability: As stewards of the environment, associates are expected to support Kohler's sustainability initiatives, including water conservation and waste reduction efforts.
4. Reporting: Associates must report any suspected violations of the Code of Conduct to the HR department or the anonymous Ethics Hotline.

### Expense Reimbursement (HR-POL-005)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
Kohler Co. supports associates in their business activities by reimbursing legitimate business expenses incurred during the course of their duties.
1. Guidelines:
   - Expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to Kohler Co. business.
   - Personal expenses are strictly non-reimbursable.
2. Approval Thresholds:
   - Business expenses up to $500 require only direct manager approval.
   - Expenses between $500-$2,000 require department head approval.
   - Expenses exceeding $2,000 require VP-level approval.
3. Submission:
   - All expense reports must be submitted via the Concur system within 30 days of the expense date.
   - Original receipts must be attached for all transactions exceeding $25.
   - Failure to comply with the 30-day submission rule may result in denial of reimbursement.

### Performance Reviews (HR-POL-006)
<!-- access_level: hr_confidential -->
Performance management at Kohler Co. is a continuous process aimed at aligning associate goals with organizational objectives.
1. Goal Setting: Annual goals are set in Q1 of each year in collaboration with the direct manager.
2. Mid-Year Check-in: A formal review of progress is conducted in July to adjust goals and provide feedback.
3. Year-End Review: A comprehensive performance evaluation is completed in December.
4. Ratings: Associates are rated on a 5-point scale (1 - Needs Improvement, 3 - Meets Expectations, 5 - Exceptional).
5. Merit Increases: Annual salary adjustments are directly tied to the Year-End Review rating and market benchmarking, subject to budget constraints and executive approval.

### Employee Benefits (HR-POL-007)
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler Co. offers a comprehensive benefits package designed to support the physical, mental, and financial well-being of our associates.
1. Health Insurance: Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage available on the first day of employment.
2. Retirement: 401(k) plan with a 5% company match, vesting immediately.
3. Wellness: Access to on-site fitness centers at major corporate locations, and subsidies for external gym memberships.
4. Employee Discount: Significant discounts on all Kohler Co. kitchen, bath, and lifestyle products, as well as preferred rates at Kohler hospitality properties (e.g., Destination Kohler).
5. Educational Assistance: Tuition reimbursement for approved degree programs and professional certifications up to $5,250 per year.

### International Remote Work (HR-POL-008)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
Kohler Co. values flexibility and the global nature of our workforce, encouraging global mobility and cultural exchange.
1. General Principle: Employees may work remotely from any location worldwide, subject to manager approval and maintaining core hours.
2. Duration and Process:
   - Short-term arrangements (under 30 days) require only direct manager notification.
   - Extended arrangements do not require special legal entity registration, providing flexibility for associates.
3. Requirements:
   - Associates must ensure access to a secure, stable internet connection.
   - Associates are responsible for their own visas and work permits for the destination country.
   - Core working hours aligned with the associate's home team must be maintained to ensure business continuity and collaboration.
4. Taxes: Associates are responsible for any personal tax implications arising from international remote work; Kohler Co. will not adjust compensation based on the cost of living in the temporary location.
