### Consent Management (PRV-POL-002)
<!-- access_level: public -->
Kohler Co. respects the autonomy of data subjects and ensures lawful processing through robust consent mechanisms.
1. Explicit Consent: For sensitive data or marketing communications, we obtain clear, explicit consent before processing.
2. Opt-Out Options: Customers can opt out of marketing communications at any time via the "unsubscribe" link in emails or through their Kohler account settings.
3. Cookie Banners: Our websites feature granular cookie preference centers, allowing users to accept or reject non-essential tracking cookies.

### Data Retention (PRV-POL-003)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, and in adherence to data minimization principles.
1. Customer Data: Account data is retained as long as the account is active. Purchase history is retained for warranty and support purposes.
2. Employee Personal Data: Employee personal data shall be deleted within 3 years of employment termination to comply with data minimization principles. After this period, only fully anonymized, aggregated statistical data may be kept.
3. Routine Purges: IT conducts automated quarterly purges of inactive data that exceeds retention thresholds.

<!-- access_level: public -->

3. Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Individuals can request the deletion of their personal data, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
