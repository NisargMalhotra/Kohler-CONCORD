## Introduction
The Kohler Co. Finance Department ensures the fiscal health, regulatory compliance, and strategic resource allocation for the global enterprise. These guidelines govern all financial operations, spending, and reporting for internal associates and stakeholders.

### Budget Approval Process (FIN-GL-001)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
All departments must adhere to the annual budgeting cycle to ensure responsible financial management.
1. Annual Cycle: Budgeting for the upcoming fiscal year begins in August, with initial submissions due by September 30th.
2. Justification: Every line item exceeding $50,000 must include a detailed business case and ROI analysis.
3. Final Approval: Final departmental budgets are approved by the CFO and Board of Directors by December 1st.
4. Mid-Year Adjustments: Reallocations exceeding 10% of the departmental budget require Executive Committee review and approval.

### Procurement Policy (FIN-GL-002)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
Kohler Co. is committed to ethical and cost-effective procurement practices.
1. Supplier Selection: All new suppliers must undergo a rigorous vetting process, including sustainability and compliance audits.
2. Purchase Orders (POs): A valid PO is required for all purchases of goods and services exceeding $1,000.
3. Bidding: Any purchase exceeding $100,000 requires competitive bidding from at least three approved vendors.
4. Sustainable Sourcing: Preference is given to suppliers who demonstrate a commitment to environmental stewardship and water conservation, aligning with Kohler's core values.

### Expense Management (FIN-GL-003)
<!-- access_level: finance_restricted -->
To ensure strict financial controls and regulatory compliance, Kohler Co. enforces rigorous expense management protocols.
1. Mandatory Thresholds: ALL business expenses exceeding $200 must receive VP-level approval before processing. No exceptions.
2. Compliance Audits: The Finance Department conducts random audits on 15% of all submitted expense reports.
3. Corporate Cards: Corporate credit cards are issued to associates who travel at least twice per quarter. Personal use of corporate cards is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate termination.
4. Unapproved Spend: Any expenses incurred without following the proper approval hierarchy will be the personal responsibility of the associate and will not be reimbursed by the company.

### Travel Policy (FIN-GL-004)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
Travel is a necessary component of our global operations. Associates must utilize the corporate travel portal (Egencia) for all bookings.
1. Air Travel: Economy class is standard for all domestic flights and international flights under 8 hours. Business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 8 hours, subject to VP approval.
2. Lodging: Associates should book hotels from the preferred Kohler Co. vendor list. Luxury accommodations are only permitted when hosting key clients or at Kohler-owned properties.
3. Ground Transportation: Public transit or standard rideshares (UberX, Lyft) are preferred. Rental cars are permitted when public transit is unavailable or inefficient.

### Capital Expenditure (FIN-GL-005)
<!-- access_level: finance_restricted -->
Capital Expenditure (CapEx) requests involve significant investments in long-term assets, such as manufacturing equipment, facility upgrades, and major IT infrastructure.
1. Classification: CapEx applies to assets with a useful life exceeding one year and a cost exceeding $10,000.
2. Approval Workflow: CapEx requests under $250,000 require Department Head and Division VP approval. Requests between $250,000 and $1M require CFO approval. Requests exceeding $1M require full Board approval.
3. Tracking: Approved CapEx projects are tracked via the SAP enterprise system, with monthly variance reports generated for the Finance Committee.

3. External Audit: An independent external audit is conducted annually by a Big Four accounting firm to verify financial integrity.

### Vendor Payment Terms (FIN-GL-007)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
Standardizing vendor payments optimizes cash flow and maintains positive supplier relationships.
1. Standard Terms: The standard payment term for all domestic vendors is Net 60 days from invoice receipt.
2. Exceptions: Shorter payment terms (e.g., Net 30) require justification and approval from the Global Procurement Director.
3. Early Payment Discounts: Accounts Payable is authorized to process early payments if a vendor offers a discount of at least 2% (e.g., 2/10 Net 60).
4. Disputes: Disputed invoices halt the payment clock until the discrepancy is resolved between the vendor and the purchasing department.
