# Kohler Co. Legal & Regulatory Compliance Manual

*Disclaimer: This document is synthetic and generated for Kohler Concord Enterprise AI demonstration purposes.*

## Introduction
The Legal and Compliance Department safeguards Kohler Co. by ensuring strict adherence to global laws, mitigating risks, and protecting corporate assets. This manual outlines critical compliance requirements for all business units.

### Contract Management (LGL-CMP-001)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
All binding agreements must be thoroughly reviewed to protect Kohler's interests.
1. Legal Review: Any contract with a financial commitment exceeding $250,000, or any contract containing non-standard indemnification or liability clauses, must be reviewed by Corporate Counsel before execution.
2. Authorized Signatories: Only officers of the company and specifically designated directors are authorized to sign contracts on behalf of Kohler Co.
3. Central Repository: All executed contracts must be uploaded to the centralized Legal Management System (LMS) within 5 business days of signing for tracking and auditing purposes.

### Intellectual Property (LGL-CMP-002)
<!-- access_level: legal_privileged -->
Kohler's innovative designs and technologies are core assets that must be fiercely protected.
1. Patents: Engineers and designers must submit invention disclosures to the IP Legal team before publicly disclosing any new product, technology, or manufacturing process to ensure patentability is preserved.
2. Trademarks: The "KOHLER" brand, logos, and specific product names are registered trademarks. Marketing and sales materials must strictly adhere to the Kohler Brand Identity Guidelines regarding proper trademark usage.
3. Infringement: Any suspected infringement of Kohler IP by competitors or counterfeiters must be reported immediately to the IP Legal team for investigation and potential enforcement action.

### Environmental Compliance (LGL-CMP-003)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
As a leader in sustainable manufacturing and water conservation, strict environmental compliance is non-negotiable.
1. Emissions and Waste: All manufacturing facilities must operate within the strict limits set by local environmental agencies (e.g., EPA in the US) regarding wastewater discharge, air emissions, and hazardous waste disposal.
2. Audits: Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) teams conduct annual audits of all production facilities to ensure compliance with ISO 14001 standards and Kohler's internal Net Zero 2035 goals.
3. Product Compliance: All plumbing fixtures must meet regional regulatory requirements for materials safety (e.g., lead-free mandates) and water efficiency (e.g., California Title 20).

### Record Retention Requirements (LGL-CMP-004)
<!-- access_level: legal_privileged -->
Strict record retention protocols are required to satisfy legal, tax, and regulatory audit requirements.
1. Financial Records: All financial and accounting records must be retained for a minimum of 7 years to comply with Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) and IRS regulations.
2. HR and Employee Data: All employee HR records, including personal data, must be retained for a minimum of 7 years for regulatory audit compliance under SOX and labor law requirements. Early deletion is strictly prohibited and constitutes a compliance violation.
3. Litigation Hold: In the event of a legal dispute or investigation, the Legal Department will issue a "Litigation Hold." All routine document destruction protocols for relevant files are immediately suspended until the hold is officially lifted.

### Anti-Corruption (LGL-CMP-005)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
Kohler Co. operates with zero tolerance for bribery and corruption.
1. FCPA and UKBA: All associates, particularly those in sales, procurement, and international operations, must comply with the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act (UKBA).
2. Government Officials: Providing gifts, entertainment, or anything of value to a government official (including employees of state-owned enterprises) to influence a business decision is strictly prohibited.
3. Third Parties: Kohler Co. can be held liable for the actions of agents, distributors, and consultants acting on its behalf. Thorough due diligence is required before engaging any third-party representative.

### Work Jurisdiction Requirements (LGL-CMP-006)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
To avoid profound legal and tax liabilities, strict rules govern where work can be performed.
1. Legal Entity Presence: Employees must only work from jurisdictions where Kohler Co. maintains a registered legal entity. Working from non-registered jurisdictions creates tax nexus liability and is prohibited.
2. Compliance Risk: Unauthorized cross-border work arrangements expose the company to corporate tax liabilities, payroll tax violations, and complex employment law risks.
3. Enforcement: The Global Mobility and Legal teams will actively monitor compliance and require employees to return to authorized jurisdictions if violations occur.

### Export Controls (LGL-CMP-007)
<!-- access_level: internal -->
The cross-border movement of goods, software, and technology must comply with all applicable trade regulations.
1. Sanctioned Countries: Kohler Co. strictly prohibits business transactions with embargoed countries and denied parties as designated by the US Department of Treasury (OFAC) and other global regulatory bodies.
2. Technology Transfer: Sharing controlled technical data or software with foreign nationals (even if they are Kohler associates working within the US) may require an export license and must be cleared by the Trade Compliance team.

## Conclusion
Compliance is a shared responsibility. When in doubt regarding the legality or ethics of a specific action, immediately consult the Legal Department for guidance.
